Common questions

What does a doula do?

A doula provides continuous emotional, physical, and informational support before, during, and after childbirth. Unlike a midwife or OB, a doula doesn't perform medical tasks — instead, they help you feel informed, calm, and supported so you can advocate for the birth you want.

How is a doula different from a midwife?

A midwife or OB provides medical care and delivers your baby. A doula does not provide medical care — they focus entirely on comfort, encouragement, and continuity of support, and can work alongside any provider or birth setting.

When should I hire a doula?

Many families reach out in their second trimester, though it's never too early — or, in many cases, too late — to ask. Booking earlier gives more time to build a relationship and a birth plan together.

Do you work with hospital births, home births, and birth centers?

Yes — I support births in hospitals, at home, and at birth centers throughout the Edmond and OKC metro area, alongside whatever provider you've chosen.

Will you replace my partner's role during labor?

No — a doula works alongside your partner or support person, not in place of them. Many partners feel more present and less anxious with a doula there to handle the physical comfort measures and pacing.

Does insurance cover doula support?

Coverage varies by plan, and some insurance and Medicaid programs have begun covering doula care. A detailed receipt is provided on request so you can submit for reimbursement or use HSA/FSA funds.

What's your backup plan if you can't make it to my birth?

Every doula should have a backup plan for the rare case they can't attend — ask about mine during your consultation.

What areas do you serve?

Edmond and the greater Oklahoma City metro. If you're outside that area, reach out anyway — travel may still be possible.

How do we get started?

Reach out for a free consultation — by phone, video, or in person. We'll talk through your due date, birth preferences, and any questions you have, with zero obligation to book.
