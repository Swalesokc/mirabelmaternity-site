My Approach

How I support families

Evidence-informed, judgment-free

Every family gets to make their own choices about their birth and how they feed their baby. My job is to help you find the information you need and support the decisions that are right for you — never to judge them.

A steady presence

During labor, that means calm, hands-on comfort measures and clear communication with your care team. Postpartum, it means practical help and reassurance while you and your baby settle in.

I'd love to meet you and your family.

A free consultation is a low-pressure way to ask questions and see if we're the right fit.
